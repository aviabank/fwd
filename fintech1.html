📅 Forward Models

4 Types of Aircraft Forward

Aircraft forward contracts come in different structures. AviaForward supports all four — each fits a different aviation financing scenario.

01
📅

Fixed-Date Forward

Standard forward

Buyer and seller agree on a price today for delivery on a specific future date. Both sides are obligated.

  • Delivery date locked
  • Price locked
  • Both parties obligated
  • Cash or physical settlement
02
🔄

Cash-Settled Forward

No physical delivery

At expiry, the difference between spot and forward price is settled in cash. Used when physical delivery is impractical.

  • No aircraft delivery
  • Cash difference paid
  • Easier to trade
  • Lower operational cost
03
🔀

Flexible-Date Forward

Window delivery

Delivery can occur within a window of dates (e.g. Q2 2028). Price is fixed, but delivery date is flexible.

  • Delivery window locked
  • Price fixed
  • More flexibility
  • Common for aircraft
04
💼

Portfolio Forward

Multiple aircraft

Forward on a basket of aircraft — e.g. 3× €B787 + 2× €A350. Used for fleet-renewal planning.

  • Multiple aircraft
  • Basket pricing
  • Fleet planning
  • Institutional size
