💡 How Governance Works

1 staked €CERT = 1 vote. Only staked €CERT counts — not free-floating tokens. This prevents governance attacks.

Quorum: 10% of total staked €CERT must participate for a proposal to pass.

Timelock: Passed proposals have 48h timelock before execution.

Execution: On-chain — parameters update automatically via smart contract.
