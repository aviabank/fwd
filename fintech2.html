⚙️ Mechanics

How AviaForward Works On-Chain

Token-2022 on Solana. Three-layer audit gate enforced by Transfer Hook.

🔗

Token-2022 Standard

Modern Solana token standard with extensions: Transfer Fee, Transfer Hook, Non-Transferable, Metadata.

💸

1% Transfer Fee

Every transfer allocates 1% to the AviaTrust treasury + insurance pool.

🪝

Transfer Hook

Custom on-chain program runs BEFORE every transfer. Verifies €PROOF validity, €CERT stake, and audit freshness.

🔒

Mint Authority

Disabled after initial mint. Fixed supply, no inflation.

❄️

Freeze Authority

Disabled. No one can freeze your tokens — but Transfer Hook can still block invalid transfers.

📜

On-Chain Metadata

Forward terms (price, expiry, aircraft token, settlement type) stored in Metaplex metadata.

🛡️ Three Audit Gates (Transfer Hook Enforced)

1
€AUDT — Burn on Creation
Every €FWD is minted only after X €AUDT has been burned for the underlying aircraft audit. No burn → no mint.
burn(AUDT, amount) → mint(FWD, forward)
2
€PROOF — Verify on Transfer
Every transfer of €FWD checks for valid €PROOF certificate. Expired certificate → transfer blocked. This prevents trading of un-audited forwards.
require(hasValidProof(sender, aircraft) == true)
3
€CERT — Stake Required
Sender must have minimum €CERT staked. Anti-spam + skin in the game.
require(stakedCERT(sender) >= minStake)
